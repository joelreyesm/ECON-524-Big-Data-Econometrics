Classroom challenge

Catch at least 80% of churners. Among thresholds that achieve this, try to flag as few customers as possible. How many offers go to customers who stayed?

This is a retrospective teaching game: flags are predictions, not evidence that an offer would prevent churn. In practice, choose a threshold using validation data and business costs, then assess it on a separate test set.
